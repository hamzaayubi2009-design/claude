# Midnight Atelier Burger

Design a premium, aesthetic, professional website for a high-end burger restaurant called “Hamza’s Cafe.”

The website should feel like a luxury contemporary burger restaurant combined with a handcrafted stop-motion animation studio. Do NOT make it look like a typical fast-food website.

CORE CREATIVE DIRECTION

Create an immersive, editorial-quality restaurant website with a sophisticated “Midnight Atelier” aesthetic.

The visual identity should combine:

luxury restaurant branding + handcrafted cotton puppet character + cinematic food photography + modern editorial web design.

The website should feel expensive, artistic, warm, memorable, and professionally designed.

COLOR PALETTE

Use a restrained premium palette:

 Deep espresso brown / almost black as the primary background

 Warm ivory / cream for major text and surfaces

 Muted copper / caramel as the main accent

 Very subtle dark olive as a secondary accent

 Occasional warm amber highlights

Avoid bright red, bright yellow, neon colors, excessive gradients, or stereotypical fast-food colors.

Use the copper accent sparingly for buttons, small details, dividers, prices, and interactive states.

TYPOGRAPHY

Use an elegant editorial serif typeface for large headlines, similar in feeling to Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, or Canela.

Pair it with a clean modern sans-serif similar to Inter, DM Sans, or Neue Haas Grotesk for navigation, descriptions, buttons, and labels.

Headlines should be large, confident, elegant, and highly readable.

HOMEPAGE HERO

Create a dramatic full-screen hero section.

Use the provided cotton/felt cartoon chef image as the main visual reference.

The chef is a charming handcrafted puppet character making a burger in a premium restaurant kitchen.

Position the chef and burger-making scene predominantly on the right side.

Keep the left side visually clean so the typography has plenty of breathing room.

Add:

HAMZA’S CAFE

Main headline:

“CRAFTED TO BE CRAVED.”

Supporting copy:

“Handcrafted burgers, bold flavors, and a little magic in every layer.”

Primary CTA:

ORDER NOW

Secondary CTA:

EXPLORE THE MENU

Add a small understated label such as:

HANDCRAFTED BURGERS · EST. 2026

The hero should feel like the opening frame of a luxury food commercial.

HERO VISUAL DIRECTION

The chef should look like he is made from:

 soft cotton

 felt

 wool

 fabric

 visible textile fibers

 subtle stitching

 soft rounded forms

 handcrafted imperfections

Do not make him look like a generic shiny 3D character.

The surrounding restaurant should use:

 dark walnut

 black stone

 brushed copper

 warm ambient lighting

 subtle shadows

 premium kitchen details

BURGER STORY SECTION

Below the hero, introduce the concept of the burger being created step-by-step.

Create a visually striking section titled:

“WATCH IT COME TOGETHER.”

Show the burger-making journey as a premium visual story:

01 — SEAR

Beef hits the hot grill.

02 — LAYER

Fresh ingredients are prepared.

03 — LEVITATE

Ingredients rise into the air.

04 — ASSEMBLE

Everything comes together into the finished burger.

This section should be designed so it can later be converted into a 3D scroll-driven animation.

MENU SECTION

Create an elegant menu section titled:

“THE HOUSE FAVORITES.”

Display 3–4 signature burgers in large editorial cards.

Each card should contain:

 premium burger image

 burger name

 short description

 price

 subtle category label

 “ORDER” interaction

Example burgers:

THE HAMZA

Double smashed beef, aged cheddar, caramelized onions, house sauce.

THE ATELIER

Smashed beef, smoked cheese, roasted mushrooms, truffle-style house sauce.

THE CLASSIC

Beef, cheddar, lettuce, tomato, pickles and signature sauce.

Keep the cards elegant and spacious rather than crowded.

INGREDIENTS SECTION

Create a premium editorial section titled:

“NOTHING ORDINARY GOES BETWEEN THE BUNS.”

Highlight:

100% Beef

Fresh Produce

House Sauce

Freshly Baked Buns

Use close-up food imagery and subtle visual details.

OUR STORY

Create a sophisticated storytelling section:

“MADE BY HAND. SERVED WITH HEART.”

Include a short restaurant story and use the cotton chef as a recurring visual character.

This section should feel intimate and handcrafted rather than corporate.

EXPERIENCE / 3D PREPARATION

Design the interface with future 3D animation in mind.

The layout should provide space for a full-screen interactive WebGL experience.

The eventual animation will show:

Chef → Beef frying → Beef levitating → Ingredients floating → Burger assembling → Finished burger

Do not design elements that would interfere with this future animation.

NAVIGATION

Create a minimal floating navigation bar:

HAMZA’S CAFE

Menu

Our Story

Experience

Contact

Right side:

ORDER NOW

Keep the navigation minimal and elegant.

On scroll, the navigation can become slightly more opaque with a subtle dark glass effect.

FOOTER

Create a sophisticated footer containing:

Hamza’s Cafe

Crafted to be craved.

Opening hours

Location

Contact

Instagram

Privacy

Terms

UI STYLE

Use:

 generous whitespace

 large editorial typography

 thin subtle borders

 sophisticated rounded corners

 restrained shadows

 subtle hover states

 elegant micro-interactions

 asymmetrical editorial layouts

 large immersive imagery

Buttons should feel premium and tactile.

Avoid excessive cards, excessive rounded containers, excessive gradients, and generic SaaS-style layouts.

ANIMATION DIRECTION

Design the interface to support future cinematic animations:

 slow image reveals

 text fade/slide transitions

 subtle parallax

 gentle image scaling

 smooth section transitions

 ingredient movement

 floating objects

Everything should feel slow, controlled, and premium.

RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Create desktop and mobile layouts.

On mobile, preserve the premium visual hierarchy and simplify the composition rather than simply shrinking the desktop design.

IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Do not make this look like a normal burger restaurant website.

The goal is to create a website that someone remembers after visiting it.

Imagine a luxury restaurant brand that hired a world-class creative studio to build an interactive website around a handcrafted cotton chef character.

The final design should feel:

CINEMATIC

LUXURIOUS

HANDCRAFTED

WARM

MINIMAL

ARTISTIC

PREMIUM

PROFESSIONAL

Generate the complete homepage design with a strong visual hierarchy and polished production-quality UI. Prioritize the hero section and the burger-making story as the defining elements of the experience.
